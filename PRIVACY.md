# Saarthi — Privacy Policy

**Developer:** Saarthi

Saarthi is a Hindi-, Hinglish-, and English-friendly Chrome browser assistant. It provides a floating assistant and Chrome side panel for user-requested browser tasks, including tab management, YouTube search/playback, and supported Instagram Reel downloads.

This policy explains how Saarthi handles information when you use the browser extension. It applies to the extension and its related command-processing service.

## 1. Information involved in providing Saarthi

**Commands and speech transcripts.** When you submit a typed command or use voice input, the resulting text may be sent to Saarthi's command-processing backend to interpret your request and return an action. Saarthi uses the browser's speech-recognition functionality for voice commands; speech processing may also involve the browser or speech-service provider according to its own policies. Do not include sensitive information in commands.

**Browser tabs and webpage content.** Saarthi can access the current tab and parts of supported webpages when necessary to show the floating assistant or perform a command you request. This may include webpage URLs, video links, titles or other content needed for a requested action. Access to a webpage does not mean every webpage is transmitted to Saarthi's backend.

**Download information.** When you request a supported Instagram Reel download, Saarthi processes the media link and asks Chrome to save the file to your device. The downloaded media is stored according to your Chrome download settings.

**Local extension settings.** Saarthi uses Chrome extension storage to keep settings such as the position of the floating assistant and information needed for an authenticated session. This may include an access token. Chrome-managed local storage is distinct from data transmitted to the command-processing backend.

**Operational information.** The backend or infrastructure provider may process technical request metadata, such as IP addresses, timestamps and diagnostic information, to provide and secure the service. The exact information logged and its retention period must be confirmed before publication.

## 2. How information is used

Saarthi processes information only to operate its browser assistant and related functions, interpret and execute your commands, maintain authentication, protect the service, and diagnose operational problems. It does not use information from the extension to determine creditworthiness or for lending decisions.

## 3. Service providers and data sharing

Saarthi may rely on a backend hosting provider and on browser-operated speech recognition to provide its functions. Content or identifiers required to carry out an action may be handled by the website or browser service involved (for example, YouTube, Instagram or Chrome Downloads). Please review the privacy policies of those services as appropriate.

**Before publication, identify the actual hosting, speech, AI-model, analytics and logging providers used in your release here, and describe which data each receives.** Do not publish this draft without completing that review.

Saarthi does not intend to sell user data or transfer it for targeted advertising. This statement must be verified against all production integrations before publication.

## 4. Permissions and site access

Saarthi requests browser permissions to provide its features:

- **`tabs`:** Find and manage browser tabs in response to commands.
- **`storage`:** Store extension preferences and authentication-related state.
- **`sidePanel`:** Display the assistant in Chrome's side panel.
- **`downloads`:** Save supported media at your request.
- **Website access (`<all_urls>`):** Display the floating assistant on supported pages and perform relevant webpage actions. Site access is broad; Saarthi should minimize the content it accesses and avoid unrelated collection.

## 5. Storage, retention and security

Extension preferences and session data may be stored in Chrome's extension storage. Commands sent to the backend travel over the connection configured by the extension. The published release must use encrypted HTTPS connections for remote services, and secrets must not be placed in public repositories.

**Before publication, state the actual server-side command/log retention periods, access controls, deletion process and whether any voice/audio recordings are retained.** These practices must reflect the deployed release rather than assumptions.

## 6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop using Saarthi at any time, withhold microphone access, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes extension-managed local data according to Chrome's behavior but does **not** necessarily delete any data already stored by a backend service. To request access to or deletion of server-held personal data, contact the developer using the support address above. The developer will respond subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

## 7. Children and sensitive information

Saarthi is a general browser productivity tool and is not designed for children. Avoid submitting passwords, financial information, health details or other sensitive information in commands or on pages where the assistant is active.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Saarthi's features and data practices change. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and providing additional notices where required.

## 9. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed through extension permissions will be used only for Saarthi's disclosed user-facing functionality and permitted related operational purposes.
